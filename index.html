How to play

  • Drag a block to slide it. Blocks can't pass through each other.
  • Push a block into a gate of the same color to clear it. The block has to fit inside the gate.
  • Blocks with white arrows only slide the way the arrows point.
  • Slide every picture piece onto its spot in the blueprint. It locks in and becomes part of the floor.
  • Finish the picture before the timer runs out.

Design choices in this prototype

  • The blueprint is normal floor. Any block can slide over it or park on it. Only the matching piece locks there.
  • Pieces lock on their exact cells and become part of the floor. Other blocks can slide over a placed piece, so placing pieces never traps anything.
  • You win when the picture is complete. Leftover blocks clear themselves first, then the picture lights up and comes to life (the rocket launches, the car drives off). You only clear what's in the way.
  • Picture pieces never leave through gates. Gates are only for extra blocks.
  • Pieces are cut along the grid, so they always fill the blueprint exactly.
  • Both levels were checked with a solver and can be completed. Every block fits at least one gate of its color.
  • Power-ups refill for free when empty (prototype only).
